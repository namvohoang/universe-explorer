## Solar System

### Our Solar System (`solar`)

*Welcome, space explorer*

- [ ] Hello: This is our neighborhood in space: one star, the Sun, with 8 planets circling it. Tap any planet or its name to fly there.
- [ ] Stat: Planets — 8
- [ ] Stat: Stars — 1, the Sun
- [ ] Stat: Age — 4.6 billion years
- [ ] Stat: Sunlight to Earth — 8 minutes

*Star · the center of it all*

- [ ] Hello: The Sun is a star, a giant glowing ball of hot gas. It gives us light and keeps us warm.

*Planet · 1st from the Sun*

## Milky Way

### The Milky Way (`galaxy`)

*Galaxy · our home*

- [ ] Hello: This is the Milky Way, a giant spinning city of stars. Our Sun is just one of them!
- [ ] Stat: Stars — 100 to 400 billion
- [ ] Stat: Width — 100,000 light-years
- [ ] Stat: Shape — Spiral
- [ ] Stat: Age — about 13 billion years

*Our Sun in the galaxy*

- [ ] Stat: To the center — 26,000 light-years
- [ ] Stat: One trip around — 230 million years
- [ ] Stat: Speed — 800,000 km/h
- [ ] Stat: Nearest star — 4.2 light-years

## Black Hole

### Black Hole (`blackhole`)

*The strongest gravity there is*

- [ ] Hello: A black hole is a place where gravity pulls so hard that nothing can escape, not even light.
- [ ] Stat: Made from — A giant star collapsing
- [ ] Stat: Light escapes? — No!
- [ ] Stat: Nearest known — 1,500 light-years
- [ ] Stat: First photo — 2019
- [ ] Fact: If you fell in, you would be stretched like a noodle. Scientists really call it spaghettification!
- [ ] Fact: The black middle is called the event horizon: the point of no return.
- [ ] Fact: Black holes do not suck things in like a vacuum cleaner. Things only fall in if they get too close.

### The Glowing Disk (`disk`)

*Accretion disk*

- [ ] Hello: Gas and dust swirl around the black hole super fast, like water around a drain. It gets so hot that it glows.
- [ ] Stat: Temperature — millions of °C
- [ ] Stat: Fastest part — half light speed
- [ ] Stat: Made of — gas and dust
- [ ] Stat: Also called — accretion disk
- [ ] Fact: A disk like this can shine brighter than a whole galaxy of stars.
- [ ] Fact: The bright ring around the black hole is light bent by gravity. The black hole works like a giant lens!
- [ ] Fact: Gas that slips too close falls in and is never seen again.
