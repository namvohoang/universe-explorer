# Universe Explorer

A click-and-explore 3D tour of space, made for kids ages 6 to 9.

- **Solar System**: tap the Sun, any of the 8 planets, or the Moon to fly there and read fun facts. Orbits run at real relative speeds (1 Earth year = 20 seconds on Normal).
- **Milky Way**: find the tiny spot where our Sun lives, and the black hole at the galaxy's center.
- **Black Hole**: a glowing accretion disk swirling around the event horizon.

Every card has a **Read it to me** button for kids who are still learning to read. Tap **Natural voice** to switch to [Kokoro](https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX), a human-sounding voice model that runs entirely in your browser (about 90 MB, downloaded once and then cached). If it can't load, the built-in browser voice reads instead.

Built with [three.js](https://threejs.org). Planet sizes and distances are squished so everything fits on one screen.
